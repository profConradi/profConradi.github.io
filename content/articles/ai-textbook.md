# Artificial Intelligence in High School Education

In 2020, together with my colleague Roberta Molinari, I embarked on the adventure of writing a textbook on artificial intelligence, finally putting into writing all the teaching experiences experimented over the previous four years with classes in the robotics track at ITIS Delpozzo in Cuneo, Italy.

## Topics Covered

The book addresses the following topics:

- **History of AI**: From the Dartmouth Conference to modern deep learning
- **Ethical and social aspects of AI**: Bias, transparency, and societal impact
- **Python programming**: Foundational skills for AI development
- **Data science libraries and tools**: NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib
- **Machine learning with scikit-learn**: Supervised and unsupervised learning algorithms

## Educational Philosophy

The textbook was designed with several pedagogical principles in mind:

### Hands-On Learning
Every concept is accompanied by practical exercises and real-world examples. Students don't just learn about AI—they build AI systems.

### Interdisciplinary Approach
AI sits at the intersection of computer science, mathematics, ethics, and philosophy. The book reflects this by integrating perspectives from multiple disciplines.

### Ethical Awareness
Throughout the text, we emphasize the ethical implications of AI technologies. Students learn not just how to build AI systems, but also when and whether they should.

## Target Audience

The book is designed for high school students in technical tracks, particularly those specializing in:

- Computer science
- Robotics
- Information technology
- Smart systems

However, the progressive structure makes it accessible to motivated learners with basic programming knowledge.

## Why Teach AI in High School?

As AI increasingly shapes our world, understanding it is no longer optional—it's essential for informed citizenship. By introducing AI education at the high school level, we:

1. **Demystify the technology**: AI is not magic, it's mathematics and algorithms
2. **Empower students**: They become creators, not just consumers, of AI
3. **Foster critical thinking**: Students learn to evaluate AI systems critically
4. **Prepare for the future**: AI literacy is becoming as fundamental as digital literacy

## Reception and Impact

Since publication, the book has been adopted by numerous technical high schools across Italy, helping hundreds of students gain practical AI skills and theoretical understanding.

The project demonstrated that complex topics like neural networks and machine learning can be made accessible to teenagers when taught with the right pedagogical approach and adequate scaffolding.
