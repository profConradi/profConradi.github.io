These works explore polynomial equations with complex parameters on the unit circle. Through computational sampling of millions of parameter pairs, unexpected mathematical geographies emerge in the complex plane: dense regions alternate with voids, fluid structures interweave in organic patterns reminiscent of biological forms.

The algorithmic process reveals how small parameter variations generate dramatic transformations in root distributions. Color, determined by parameter angles, encodes precise mathematical information—transforming numerical data into visual language.

These patterns are not drawn but *discovered*, emerging naturally from the underlying algebraic structure. Python code becomes an instrument of revelation, allowing us to observe symmetries and complexities that would otherwise remain confined to the abstraction of equations.
